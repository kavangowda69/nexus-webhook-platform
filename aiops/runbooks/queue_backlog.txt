RUNBOOK: QUEUE_BACKLOG

Symptoms:
- Queue depth exceeding 5000 jobs
- Delivery latency p95 above 400ms
- Queue depth rate increasing

Root Causes:
- Worker pod saturation (insufficient replicas)
- Slow downstream endpoints causing worker blocking
- Redis connection pool exhaustion

Remediation Steps:
1. Scale worker deployment up by 2 replicas
2. Check downstream endpoint health via circuit breaker metrics
3. If scaling does not resolve within 5 minutes, escalate to on-call

Cooldown: 300 seconds between scaling actions
Max replicas: 8
